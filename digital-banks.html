Best Fee-Free UK Digital Banks

Budgeting Tools  |  5 min read  |  Updated 2026

Digital banking apps have changed how students manage money. Instant notifications, spending pots and card controls in your pocket make it far easier to stay on top of a tight budget. Here is what to look for and how the best-known fee-free options compare.

What to look for in a student-friendly app

  • No monthly fee. A basic current account should not cost you anything to run.
  • Protection for your money. Check whether the provider is a bank covered by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS) or an e-money provider that safeguards your funds. The two work differently. See fscs.org.uk for the current limit.
  • Budgeting tools. Pots, spending categories, weekly limits and round-ups help you stick to a plan.
  • Low fees abroad. This matters if you travel or are an international student.
  • Good support and security. Look for in-app help, card freezing and strong login protection.

Digital bank or traditional student account?

Many high street banks offer student accounts with an interest-free overdraft and sometimes a welcome perk. Most digital banks do not offer a student-specific overdraft. A popular approach is to use a student account for its overdraft and a digital app for day-to-day budgeting, but only borrow if you can repay it. Remember that an overdraft is still borrowing.

Stay safe

  • Turn on two-factor authentication and use a strong, unique password.
  • Never share one-time codes or your PIN, even with someone who says they are from your bank.
  • Be wary of unexpected messages that ask you to move money to a "safe account". That is a common scam.
  • Keep your phone's software up to date.

Making your choice

The best app is the one you will actually use. If budgeting is your goal, pick one with pots and clear spending insights. If you travel often, prioritise low foreign fees. Whatever you choose, check the current fees, protection and terms on the provider's own website before you switch, and try our savings calculator to set a weekly plan.
