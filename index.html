Student Hacks

How to live on £50 a week in London

Sample article layout. Replace with your own original content.

Living in London on a tight budget sounds impossible, yet thousands of students manage it every year. The trick is not to cut everything, but to know which costs you can control and which ones you cannot.

Start with your fixed costs. Rent usually takes the biggest bite, so sharing a house and checking whether bills are included can change your whole week. Once the fixed costs are covered, the rest becomes a simple weekly pot.

Three habits that make the biggest difference

  • Plan your meals before you shop, so you only buy what you will actually eat.
  • Use student discounts and railcards, and check them before every journey.
  • Give yourself a small weekly fun budget so you are not tempted to overspend.

Use the calculator above to test your own numbers and see how small changes add up over a month.

Quick money tips

  • Check your student account for a 0% overdraft and know the limit.
  • Move spare cash into a savings pot on payday, not at the end of the month.
  • Review subscriptions every term and cancel what you do not use.
